A PRIVATE, SELF-HOSTED APP

My Fitbit Dashboard

A personal view of selected sleep, health, and running summaries from a Google Health-linked Fitbit account.

This app is configured for one owner and their own household devices. It has no public registration or multi-user service.

AT A GLANCE

What it displays

Sleep

Most recent non-nap sleep duration and an average across available sleep sessions from the last seven local dates.

Health metrics

Daily heart-rate variability, sleeping respiratory rate, and resting heart rate when Google Health provides them.

Running

Running distance for the current local week and consecutive days with a running activity.

WHERE IT RUNS

On the owner’s devices

The companion runs on the owner’s own computer or server and sends selected summaries to an authenticated local dashboard and, when configured, a CYD display. Google receives the OAuth authorization and Google Health API requests needed to provide the connection.
